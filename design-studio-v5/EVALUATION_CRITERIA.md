# Reference audition criteria — declared before V5 discovery

Candidate roles are **not** assigned at discovery. Score dimensions separately on 0–4 with a cited observation and evidence location; a score without evidence is `UNASSESSED`. Do not average away a severe weakness. A candidate can be excellent for one narrow role without being a quality bar.

## Visual dimensions

1. Authorship and visual confidence: clear choices that could not be moved unchanged to another brand.
2. Distinctiveness without mannerism: character with a reason in the product.
3. Cross-section and cross-page coherence: related layouts, typography and image behavior without monotony.
4. Typography: display, body, caption, utility and state hierarchy all considered.
5. Image direction: subject, crop, sequence, light and material consistency.
6. Layout intelligence: proportion, density, rhythm, asymmetry and useful whitespace.
7. Interaction and motion: purposeful, optional, responsive and nonblocking.
8. Mobile transformation: independently legible and composed; more than desktop stacking.
9. Distance from common AI-builder patterns: no automatic giant sans hero, decorative micro-labels, repeated cards, arbitrary accent panels or formulaic split sections.

## Hospitality and product dimensions

10. Desire to stay: rooms and lived experience feel worth a visit.
11. Property comprehension: what it is, where it is and what makes it different can be inferred quickly.
12. Room discovery and comparison: useful differences and path to detail.
13. Food/experience depth: food, place and activities have credible narrative weight.
14. Sense of place: architecture, material, landscape and local context cohere.
15. Trust and practical information: location, policies and next steps are accessible and honest.

## CRO and UX dimensions

16. High-intent path: Rooms and stay/enquiry/booking are reachable without unnecessary exploration.
17. CTA scent: labels predict what happens next.
18. Friction and decision count: each step asks only for necessary input.
19. Room decision support: users can compare and keep context.
20. Mobile conversion: important actions and form states work with thumb and keyboard.
21. Form/booking quality: errors, expectations, privacy and final state are clear.
22. Performance awareness: media, font and interaction choices do not delay the action path.

## Evidence dimensions and vetoes

Record first-party status, current URL and date, source path, desktop/mobile render-complete statuses, pages actually inspected, interaction states, screenshot/session paths and external material limits. **No live site becomes positive visual evidence without `VISUAL_CAPTURE_COMPLETE` on the relevant viewports.** HTTP 200 or an old raw screenshot is insufficient. A quality-bar candidate also needs multiple inspected pages. A serious mobile failure, generic visual system, opaque booking path or missing core evidence can veto a broad role; a narrow atomic observation may still survive with its scope stated.

## Audition procedure

First collect a diverse 40–80 URL candidate pool from product-derived queries. Fast-audition only genuinely captured sites and mark all others `UNASSESSED` or `INCOMPLETE`. Seek 12–20 deeply inspected references across different visual clusters before assigning roles. Keep visual quality, hospitality value, CRO value and evidence strength as separate columns. Do not privilege a site because an older brief named it.
