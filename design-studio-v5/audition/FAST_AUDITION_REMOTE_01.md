# V5 fast audition addendum — one newly qualified homepage

The first GitHub-hosted V5 pass added a complete desktop/mobile **home-only** capture for `CALILE` on 4 October 2026, [run 37224667588](https://github.com/GeorgiMutafchiev/kamen-house-design-experiment/actions/runs/37224667588). This is a compact first audition under the [predeclared criteria](../EVALUATION_CRITERIA.md), alongside the earlier [five-home audition](FAST_AUDITION.md). It does not assign a reference role or infer a booking funnel. The original pool remains 19; deep multi-page auditions remain zero.

| Sunlit urban-resort editorial with illustration | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | Keep only as a provisional question about image-led hotel storytelling and early booking access; scale and location differ strongly from KAMEN. |

- **Visual:** The aerial pool opening, restrained warm lettering, changing room/lobby/pool image sizes and occasional illustration create a consistent sunlit resort world. The desktop page then repeats narrow text beside photographs across several day-part sections. The mobile sequence becomes a long vertical image/text story with some small text and large gaps; this is composed but not clearly a quality bar for a small mountain house. [Desktop full page](../../design-intelligence/references/remote-evidence/37224667588/CALILE/home-desktop/attempt-01/99-full-page-after-traversal.jpg) · [mobile full page](../../design-intelligence/references/remote-evidence/37224667588/CALILE/home-mobile/attempt-01/99-full-page-after-traversal.jpg).
- **Hospitality:** The pool, rooms, interior gathering spaces and Brisbane skyline communicate a real urban resort. The opening speaks through atmosphere and the hotel mark, with room distinctions deferred until a later rooms/suites link. Its product scale and climate cannot be transplanted to KAMEN's eleven-room Rhodopes concept.
- **CRO entry:** `BOOKINGS` is visible at the upper right on both initial viewports, and `CURATE YOUR STAY` appears on desktop hero. This gives a high-intent entry but does not show room comparison, dates, rate/policy transparency, form state or completion. The mobile hero does not show `CURATE YOUR STAY` in its initial viewport. The booking destination was not clicked in this capture.
- **Evidence:** Both 1440 × 1000 and 390 × 844 sessions report `VISUAL_CAPTURE_COMPLETE`; the worker scrolled 10 sampled positions in each, document height grew, and initial/scroll/bottom/final screenshots were preserved with diagnostics and file hashes. This certifies only the observed home render, not unseen routes or commercial outcomes. [Desktop session](../../design-intelligence/references/remote-evidence/37224667588/CALILE/home-desktop/session.json) · [mobile diagnostics](../../design-intelligence/references/remote-evidence/37224667588/CALILE/home-mobile/diagnostics.json).

For a deep audition, inspect Rooms/Suites, room detail, Bookings/date selection and relevant mobile menu states, then evaluate how the flow retains a room choice. Until then, no hospitality UX, CRO, mobile or quality-bar role is earned.
