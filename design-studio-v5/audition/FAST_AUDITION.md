## Scope and evidence rule

All five first-party-looking domains were actually captured on 2026-10-04 at 1440 × 1000 desktop and 390 × 844 mobile. Each imported session reports `VISUAL_CAPTURE_COMPLETE`, a real traversal, and no browser errors or readiness warnings. Screenshots and diagnostics were inspected. These are **home only**; current live reachability and ownership were not reverified in this pass. A render-complete status establishes the observed rendered state, not that every ancillary request succeeded or that an unvisited flow works.

The earlier [scout report](../discovery/SCOUT_REPORT.md) froze at 18 of the target 40–80 leads. The [candidate CSV](../discovery/candidates.csv) now includes the subsequently imported Newt lead, yielding 19 listed URLs and a remaining 21–61-lead gap. Five homepages have fast-audition evidence; **zero** references have the deep multi-page inspection required for role assignment. Scores below use 0–4, where 4 is strong *on this homepage*, and `U` means unassessed. They are separate judgments, not a combined rank. In the CRO column, only **entry-path clarity** is scored. Room decisions, booking, forms, policies, cross-page coherence, and real conversion outcomes remain `U` for every site.

## Candidate observations

### St. John — eliminate this homepage

- **Visual strength / weakness (1):** A drawn pig mark, serif utility language, and restrained black-and-white chrome have recognizable restaurant character. The captured page is essentially one dining-room photograph followed immediately by newsletter, social and contact footer. There is too little composition, image sequencing, food depiction or hierarchy to support a broad visual study. [Desktop](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/STJOHN/sessions/home-desktop/attempt-01/99-full-page-after-traversal.jpg) · [mobile](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/STJOHN/sessions/home-mobile/attempt-01/99-full-page-after-traversal.jpg).
- **Hospitality / product (1):** A set dining room conveys a real place to eat, and the header offers Restaurants, Bakery and Winery. The home view does not explain a stay, a food point of view, or practical dining choices; lodging is outside this site's apparent product. For KAMEN's fire-kitchen question, a restaurant route would need its own capture.
- **CRO entry (2):** `RESERVATIONS` is immediately visible on desktop and mobile; mobile also keeps `BASKET (0)` in the top strip. The home view gives no decision support before that action, and the large newsletter form becomes the main content. Reservation destination and form behavior are `U`.
- **Evidence (2):** Both home sessions completed, but the document is only about 1,166 px high. Diagnostics show failed Shopify analytics/ancillary requests and one initial desktop Instafeed loader image; they do not justify treating the visible sparse layout as a rendering failure. [Desktop session](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/STJOHN/sessions/home-desktop/session.json) · [mobile diagnostics](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/STJOHN/sessions/home-mobile/diagnostics.json).

### David Chipperfield Architects — retain only for a narrow later question

- **Visual strength / weakness (3):** A quiet sans hierarchy and changing image width/alignment make a long project-and-news index feel edited rather than card-generated. Captions distinguish news, project and research. On mobile, the irregular desktop field becomes a long single-image stream with large gaps; the transformation is legible but loses much of the spatial rhythm. [Desktop](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/DAVIDCHIPPERFIELD/sessions/home-desktop/attempt-01/99-full-page-after-traversal.jpg) · [mobile](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/DAVIDCHIPPERFIELD/sessions/home-mobile/attempt-01/99-full-page-after-traversal.jpg).
- **Hospitality / product (0):** Architecture and materials are relevant as *topics*, but this homepage is a practice portfolio/news index. It supplies no guest proposition, room, food or stay task. Its architectural image handling can only be evaluated for KAMEN after a project page is inspected.
- **CRO entry (1):** Practice, Projects, People, News and Contact provide clear portfolio destinations. The mobile floating `Menu` appears down the page. There is no analogous hospitality high-intent action; this is a category-fit limit, not a claim of bad architecture-site conversion.
- **Evidence (2):** Desktop and mobile traversals are complete, with 24 images and no failed resource or browser error recorded. No project route or interaction was inspected. [Desktop session](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/DAVIDCHIPPERFIELD/sessions/home-desktop/session.json) · [mobile diagnostics](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/DAVIDCHIPPERFIELD/sessions/home-mobile/diagnostics.json).

### Casa Bonay — provisional, with a comprehension risk

- **Visual strength / weakness (3):** The warm cream desktop rail and tight asymmetrical mosaic join bath, sea, food, cycling, interiors and rooms into a lively lived-place portrait. Mobile turns that mosaic into a deliberate sequence of large photographs rather than squeezing columns. The near absence of explanatory captions means the visuals carry too much of the information task; the overall long image stack becomes repetitive. [Desktop](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/CASABONAY/sessions/home-desktop/attempt-01/99-full-page-after-traversal.jpg) · [mobile](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/CASABONAY/sessions/home-mobile/attempt-01/99-full-page-after-traversal.jpg).
- **Hospitality / product (2):** Hotel, Rooms and Food & Drink are explicit in the desktop rail, and the images show distinct social, food, outdoor and bedroom experiences. Yet the home view does not say what room types exist, what makes the hotel different in words, or how the experiences fit together. Mobile hides those categories in the hamburger at first view.
- **CRO entry (2):** Desktop shows a prominent red `Book Now!` in the persistent rail; mobile eventually presents it as a red overlay beside a blue events control. Its visibility competes with photography, while the initial mobile viewport offers only the menu. Booking destination, comparison, policies and final state are `U`.
- **Evidence (2):** Both home traversals completed and show the photo sequence. Failed requests in diagnostics are advertising, analytics and optimization endpoints, with no captured browser errors; there is no basis to infer a broken booking path. [Desktop session](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/CASABONAY/sessions/home-desktop/session.json) · [mobile diagnostics](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/CASABONAY/sessions/home-mobile/diagnostics.json).

### Fogo Island Inn — strong provisional deep-audition candidate

- **Visual strength / weakness (3):** Coastal light, sea, stilted inn, food, outdoor work and local craft build a consistent geographic world. The first screen earns its photographic scale. Further down, many equal-size experience/news image cards flatten hierarchy, especially as a long mobile stack; the soft sans also gives some utility copy limited distinction. [Desktop](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/FOGO_V4/sessions/home-desktop/attempt-01/99-full-page-after-traversal.jpg) · [mobile](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/FOGO_V4/sessions/home-mobile/attempt-01/99-full-page-after-traversal.jpg).
- **Hospitality / product (3):** The homepage names an all-inclusive inn, 29 rooms, food and drink, island activities and community purpose. Room interiors and meals appear in context, giving more substance than a scenery-only hero. KAMEN should not inherit this property's scale, claims, or all-inclusive model; this is an observation of clarity, not a design instruction.
- **CRO entry (3):** `Reserve` is visible in the desktop and mobile header. `Select Your Dates` appears immediately after the hero proposition, and `See rates & inclusions` accompanies the room statement. These labels give good next-step scent on home; actual date selector, rates and booking path remain `U`.
- **Evidence (2):** Both home traversals completed, and the recorded real `#consent-essential-only` clicks removed the consent overlay. Diagnostics include aborted analytics and MP4 requests, although visible media was captured; motion reliability and performance cannot be concluded from stills. [Desktop session](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/FOGO_V4/sessions/home-desktop/session.json) · [mobile diagnostics](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/FOGO_V4/sessions/home-mobile/diagnostics.json).

### The Newt in Somerset — provisional, with scale and rhythm risks

- **Visual strength / weakness (2):** Seasonal woodland, produce, dining interior and estate imagery make a coherent country-estate world; serif headlines and a fine-lined utility layer are controlled. After the hero, the desktop repeatedly alternates half-page image and text slabs, and mobile serializes them into a long centered sequence. The repeated pattern risks the generic editorial hospitality template described in V5 despite polished assets. [Desktop](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/NEWT_V5/sessions/home-desktop/attempt-01/99-full-page-after-traversal.jpg) · [mobile](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/NEWT_V5/sessions/home-mobile/attempt-01/99-full-page-after-traversal.jpg).
- **Hospitality / product (3):** The home view explains a working estate in Somerset with gardens, orchards, seasonal food, workshops and rooms. It also exposes membership, table booking, shopping and other estate products; these add real depth but create a proposition much larger and more complex than KAMEN's small house.
- **CRO entry (3):** `BOOK A STAY` appears over the hero and `Book a Room` in the utility strip just below; mobile repeats them early, with a further room link in the three-image row. This is good entry availability. The competing member/table/shop actions and small light hero labels may dilute priority, especially on moving imagery. Downstream stay path is `U`.
- **Evidence (2):** Both home traversals completed after recorded `Allow all cookies` clicks. Document height grew during traversal and full-page captures contain the revealed sections. Diagnostics include a third-party HubSpot form request returning 403 and ancillary video/analytics failures; the newsletter form and motion quality cannot be scored from this record. Hero imagery differs between captured desktop/mobile moments, consistent with time-varying media. [Desktop session](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/NEWT_V5/sessions/home-desktop/session.json) · [mobile diagnostics](../../design-intelligence/references/local-evidence/NEWT_V5/sessions/home-mobile/diagnostics.json).

St. John's captured home is eliminated for broad deep audition because its visible content is too thin for the KAMEN hospitality and visual questions. The other four are **questions worth testing**, not selections: Fogo for whether the clear place/stay opening holds across rooms and reservation; Casa Bonay for whether its authored collage leads to usable room information; The Newt for whether a broad estate still offers a coherent mobile stay journey; David Chipperfield for whether project pages reveal useful architectural storytelling beyond a long index.
