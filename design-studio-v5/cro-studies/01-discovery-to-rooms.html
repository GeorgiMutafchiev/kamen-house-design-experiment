Task comparison

User intent
A first-time visitor wants to identify the property and find accommodation.
Required information
Property category; Rhodope/Bulgaria clue; eleven-room scale; clear Rooms destination; concept status.
Decision and click count
Current: 1 route click to Rooms from the header or home register. Proposed: 1 route click. The clarity change is wording and context, not fewer clicks.
Trust
Generated images and fictional property status must remain apparent before a visitor treats details as travel facts.
Friction to investigate
Whether the first screen gives sufficient category and concept cues is untested; the visible “Plan a stay” action can outrun that understanding.
Mobile behavior
At small widths, the visible Stay action remains; Rooms is in the menu until opened. The storyboard tests whether an unfamiliar visitor finds that menu route.
Limitations
No participant has attempted this task. The storyboard does not define a homepage composition or establish which entry label performs better.
