Task comparison

User intent
A family of three wants to shortlist a room that fits their party.
Required information
Sleep capacity; bed arrangement; floor/steps; bathroom; room note; absence of rates and live availability.
Decision and click count
Current: from /rooms, at least 1 detail click for a candidate; 3 or more route clicks to inspect two non-adjacent candidates and return. Proposed: 1 detail click after an index-level shortlist. These are modelled route counts, not measured behavior.
Trust
Every room is a concept; occupancy and bed details are narrative specifications, not bookable inventory. Show no live availability or rate implication.
Friction to investigate
The register does not expose `sleeps` in its row although that field exists in content. A family may need repeated detail visits to find viable choices.
Mobile behavior
Compare the same core fields within each mobile row without requiring side-to-side memory or horizontal scrolling; no mobile behavior has been user tested.
Limitations
The prototype demonstrates an information order to test, not a finished room selector. It has no prices, real inventory, or observed task success.
