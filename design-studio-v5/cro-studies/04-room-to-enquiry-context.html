Task comparison

User intent
A visitor who chose a room wants to ask about dates without re-entering which room they chose.
Required information
Chosen room identity; dates; party size; honest preview outcome; a way to edit a choice.
Decision and click count
Current: 1 click detail → /stay, then manual recall and typing to preserve room choice. Proposed: 1 click and 0 room re-entry. No real enquiry is sent in either storyboard.
Trust
The selected room is a preference only; it does not imply availability, a hold, a rate, or a sent request.
Friction to investigate
The current link discards the room slug and the form has no room field. This is a code-observed context loss, not a measured abandonment.
Mobile behavior
Keep the chosen room visible near date fields and allow editing without a long return path; keyboard entry should not be required just to repeat the room name.
Limitations
This is an isolated state-carrying prototype. It does not modify the app, save data, or imply that a booking service exists.
