# Existing local task flows — separate from external funnel evidence

Scope: source inspection of the experimental KAMEN app on 4 October 2026. This is an audit of implemented routes and states, **not** a real-user or competitor funnel test. Source anchors are listed so each observation can be rechecked. Existing `tests/e2e/site.spec.ts` covers route navigation, menu use, form states, mobile overflow, and target sizing, but those tests were **not executed as part of this CRO audit**.

| Intent / route sequence | Observed implementation | Friction or evidence gap to study |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discover what the place is: `/` → `/house`, `/food`, `/around` | Home opening names eleven rooms above a fire kitchen and gives a Rhodope location clue. Separate sections link to house, food, landscape, rooms, and `/stay` (`app/page.tsx`). Header exposes these routes; mobile folds six informational routes behind a menu while retaining a visible Stay link (`app/ui/site-header.tsx`, `app/globals.css:216–240`). | Product comprehension and the first destination choice have not been tested with visitors. Food details and walking notes are narrative concept facts, not operating promises. |
| Compare rooms: `/` → `/rooms` → `/rooms/[slug]` | Home shows five of eleven room rows and a “See all eleven rooms” link. Rooms index shows all eleven with name, outlook, bed, and note; room detail adds occupancy, floor, bathroom, sound, and two paragraphs (`app/page.tsx`, `app/rooms/page.tsx`, `app/rooms/[slug]/page.tsx`, `lib/content.ts`). | Index omits sleep capacity in its main row, while room detail contains accessibility and sound traits important to a decision. There is no observed user evidence that the current information is enough for comparing all eleven rooms. |
| High-intent enquiry: `/` or header → `/stay` | “Plan a stay” is visible in the home opening and header; `/stay` immediately says the form transmits nothing and reserves no room (`app/page.tsx`, `app/ui/site-header.tsx`, `app/stay/page.tsx`). | The same action wording may create a stronger expectation than the actual preview outcome. This is an information-scent hypothesis, not observed confusion. Rates, availability, response time, contact channel, and reservation mechanism are absent by product truth. |
| Chosen room → enquiry: `/rooms/[slug]` → `/stay` | The room detail has an “Open inquiry preview” link near previous/next room links (`app/rooms/[slug]/page.tsx`). | The link has no room identifier in its URL, and `InquiryForm` has no room field or stored selection (`app/ui/inquiry-form.tsx`). A visitor must remember and type the room preference in free text. This is a concrete context-retention gap. |
| Form entry → validation → final state: `/stay` | Form asks arrival, departure, adults, children, name, email, optional message, and acknowledgement. Native required/email validation runs; custom checks reject past arrival and departure on/before arrival. A 650 ms simulated “Checking…” state ends with an explicit non-transmission message (`app/ui/inquiry-form.tsx`). | Minimum two-night stay appears beside the form but is not enforced by the custom date check. The “success” state confirms format, not delivery. No external confirmation, acknowledgement, copy/export, or recovery from leaving the page is implemented. These are product limitations, not a failed live booking. |
| Practical trust check: `/rooms` or `/stay` → `/find-us`, `/privacy` | Rooms and stay show conceptual arrival, food, and minimum-stay details. Find Us explicitly lacks a verified map pin and describes an imagined approach; footer links to Directions and Privacy (`app/rooms/page.tsx`, `app/stay/page.tsx`, `app/find-us/page.tsx`, `app/ui/site-footer.tsx`). | No real address, policies, prices, transfer arrangement, accessibility verification, or live contact destination exists. The visitor cannot independently verify practical suitability for actual travel. |

### Mobile and error-state boundary

The code shows a sticky mobile header with a visible Stay link and a menu toggle; Escape closes the menu and restores focus (`app/ui/site-header.tsx`, `app/globals.css:216–240`). This documents the intended mechanics, not observed usability. The form's status region uses `role="status"` and `aria-live="polite"` (`app/ui/inquiry-form.tsx`). The local tests describe intended checks, but live mobile task completion, keyboard error recovery, screen reader experience, and load performance remain unmeasured here.

### Six later task studies suggested by the audit

These are study briefs, not completed prototypes or results: (1) discovery visitor names the property and chooses Rooms; (2) family of three compares fit across the register; (3) mobility-constrained visitor checks room and final approach; (4) selected-room visitor retains their choice at enquiry; (5) mobile visitor corrects invalid dates and finds minimum stay; (6) visitor explains exactly what happens after “Check inquiry.” Each should record success, hesitation, misinterpretation, and needed information without implying a real reservation can be made.
