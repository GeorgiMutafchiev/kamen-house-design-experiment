# Visitor intent and conversion narrative

This is a **task model for later evaluation**, not homepage design or a claimed optimized funnel. It follows `../PRODUCT_TRUTH.md`, the local route audit, and the bounded external principles in `RESEARCH_INVENTORY.md`.

## Intent model

| Intent | Immediate question | Information needed before an honest next action | Local path to examine | Failure signal to watch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High: ready to consider a stay | “Which room fits, and can I ask about my dates?” | Room capacity, bed, floor/access, date requirements, practical arrival, clear explanation of enquiry outcome. | Visible Rooms and Stay entry; room detail; enquiry preview. | Mistaking the preview for availability or a sent request; forgetting a chosen room; hitting date rules only after submission. |
| Medium: attracted, still deciding | “Is this place right for our trip?” | Differences among rooms, food arrangement and dietary limits, walking and weather, house scale, location, approach, policy and trust facts. | House / Rooms / Food / Around / Find Us, then a room or Stay. | Emotional story outrunning practical information; inability to compare eleven rooms; unsupported operational claims. |
| Low: discovery | “What is KAMEN HOUSE and where is it?” | Clear property category, Rhodope/Bulgaria clue, eleven-room scale, fire-kitchen identity, routes to explore. | Opening comprehension, then Rooms / House / Food / Around. | Ambiguous category or location; unclear difference between reading and acting. |

These intents are hypotheses about likely visitor goals; no KAMEN audience interviews, search logs, or traffic data establish their share or priority. The primary eventual commercial action is a **truthful stay enquiry**. Within the concept site, successful behavior can only mean understanding the offer, choosing relevant information, and reaching a clearly labelled local enquiry preview. A form-valid state is not a lead, request, or reservation.

## Jobs the content sequence must do

1. **Comprehension:** establish the kind and scale of place, broad location, and the link between rooms, fire kitchen, and outdoors. A visitor should not need to infer that it is accommodation from imagery alone.
2. **Decision path:** reveal the Rooms and Stay routes early enough for a ready visitor; make each label predict its destination (S1). Keep discovery routes available for a visitor who needs context.
3. **Desire and differentiation:** explain what living in the house, eating there, and stepping outside would be like, while marking concept details honestly. This is the property's own proposition, not a borrowed conversion claim.
4. **Room decision support:** give comparable attributes before asking for an enquiry, then preserve the room choice if the visitor takes the next action. Compare capacity, bed, floor/access, outlook, and sound when those differences matter.
5. **Trust and practical fit:** surface location certainty, arrival, food needs, mobility limits, length of stay, and the absence of live rates/availability at the point they affect a decision. Do not imply verified operations where there are none.
6. **Primary action and final state:** state what the enquiry preview checks and what it does **not** do, before and after submission. Ask only for information needed to demonstrate that preview; make errors recoverable (S2 is a prompt to inspect effort, not a prediction of hotel conversion).
7. **Mobile continuity:** keep content, route labels, task context, form feedback, and action access usable on a small screen. Validate actual mobile field experience using LCP, INP, and CLS where real traffic becomes available (S3).

### Conversion thesis for the concept

**A visitor should understand the imagined mountain house quickly, compare the room and practical details relevant to their stay, and reach an honestly described enquiry preview without losing their decision context.**

The eventual operating site's funnel cannot be specified beyond that without verified availability, rates, booking/enquiry operations, policies, and visitor behavior. Any claim that this sequence converts better requires real measured outcomes after a real service exists.
