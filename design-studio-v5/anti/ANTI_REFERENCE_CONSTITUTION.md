# V5 anti-reference constitution — negative evidence only

The owner rejected both the earlier House Register direction and the later Spatial direction. That verdict is authoritative. The visual fingerprints below are **studio diagnoses of saved renders**, not additional preferences attributed to the owner. They are regression questions for future candidates, not a new design recipe. Source renders and records: [`HUMAN_REJECTED_BASELINE_001`](../../design-intelligence/research/reference-ledger.json), [`HUMAN_REJECTED_VISUAL_DIRECTION_002`](spatial/reference.json), [House Register desktop](../../tests/visual/golden/home-1440.png), [Spatial desktop](spatial/home-1440.png), and their corresponding 390 px mobile images.

| Negative source / pattern category | Visible fingerprint | UX or CRO consequence to test | Detection rule for later candidates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Both rejected directions — generic hospitality premise | The same verbal hook, eleven rooms above/around a fire kitchen, carries both first screens, and the same exterior concept image anchors the introduction. | Repetition can make a fresh visual treatment feel like a reskin of the rejected family; it can leave room and stay decisions underexplained. | In a blind first-five-seconds review, flag any opening whose composition and proposition can be overlaid on either rejected render despite new type or color. Require a documented product-derived reason for the chosen emphasis. |
| House Register — catalog metaphor over lived hospitality | A long text-only room register precedes individual room imagery; labels and ruled rows dominate the middle of the page. | A visitor may learn names before seeing meaningful differences, conditions, and reasons to stay. | Ask whether a room choice can be made from evidence visible at the decision point; flag rows that require opening every room to learn its distinguishing quality. |
| Spatial — oversized sans headline and numbered editorial labels | A very large two-line headline, tiny numbered section captions, and wide unused bands repeat down the desktop capture. | The page can signal a design portfolio more strongly than a lived small house; utility information becomes visually quiet. | Flag giant type, micro-labels, or whitespace that cannot be justified by actual visitor comprehension, desire, or a clear task. |
| Spatial — arbitrary color-field emphasis | A rust full-width plan row and a dark kitchen panel interrupt otherwise pale sections, while the relationship between emphasis and visitor decision is unclear from the screenshot. | Color contrast may create momentary drama without helping the visitor choose a room, understand food, or enquire. | For each large accent field, require an observable hierarchy or task benefit; compare the page with the accent removed. |
| Both — concept imagery as apparent property evidence | Exterior, room, kitchen, and trail images are generated concept studies, shown with small disclosure captions. Some room/image claims and architectural continuity are unverified by the asset audit. | Visitors can mistake atmospheric concept scenes for a real, bookable property; visual confidence can exceed factual confidence. | Keep disclosures legible and adjacent. Reject any design that implies verified rooms, views, architecture, availability, price, or operation from these images. Check image continuity against the separate asset audit. |
| Both — section-by-section formula | Hero/exterior, house statement, rooms, dark food block, trail/place and footer follow a similar sequence in the two saved home renders. | A section list can look complete while leaving the high-intent path and persuasion sequence mechanically assembled. | Blind-compare full-page rhythm with both rejected captures. If only surface treatment changed, reject the candidate as a continuation. Require each section's job to be explicit and evidenced. |

Other V5 anti-pattern categories remain **screening rules**, not observed external anti-references: generic AI-builder cards or grids, overdesigned portfolios, opaque booking paths, hidden primary actions, fake urgency, and intrusive overlays. No outside site is entered here as an anti-reference without a captured, cited failure. A later critic may add one only with exact URL, route, capture, and an observed consequence.
