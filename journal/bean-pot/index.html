Field note · 02 September

The bean pot stays close to the fire

A working note on low heat, late peppers and dinner for a full house.

The beans are soaked after breakfast and put close to the embers before lunch. They need time more than attention.

Late peppers go onto the hot plate until their skins blacken. The pot takes smoke from the stove door opening and closing around it.

Low heat, long afternoon

On a full-house night, the same pot reaches the table with bread, sheep’s cheese and chopped parsley.

Nothing is held in reserve for photographs. Dinner is served once, at 19:30.
