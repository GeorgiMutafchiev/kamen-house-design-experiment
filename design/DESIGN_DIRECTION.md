# Design Direction — The House Register

DESIGN_STATUS = LOCKED

## Brand feeling

Quiet, precise, tactile, and hospitable. The house feels inhabited and locally known. Confidence comes from useful knowledge rather than luxury claims.

## Compositional logic

A 12-column register pairs broad narrative or image fields with narrow factual margins. Page openings are off-center and direct. Numbering organizes rooms and chapters. Horizontal rules and baseline alignment create structure. Density changes with purpose: arrival breathes, residence unfolds, utility becomes compact.

## Typography

A Cyrillic-capable literary serif carries narrative and decisive display moments. A narrow, sturdy grotesk carries navigation, captions, figures, directions, and form UI. Sentence case is standard. Large type appears selectively. Hairline luxury serifs, generic neutral sans, and letterspaced micro-labels are forbidden.

## Image behavior

Documentary and materially specific, using approach, architectural whole, human activity, and detail. Images act as evidence. Captions say what, where, or when. Concept images are labeled honestly. Repeated stock-hotel thumbnails are forbidden.

## Spacing and navigation

Spacing is measured but not uniform: quiet openings, denser facts, and deliberate reading measures. A slim, persistent masthead exposes primary destinations. The stay action is distinct through type and rule, not a capsule. Mobile uses a clear Menu control and keeps inquiry reachable without covering content.

## Interaction and motion

Text links, rules, underlines, and slight image shifts make states visible. Motion is quick and physical. No smooth-scroll hijack, parallax theater, looping decoration, or delayed reading. Reduced motion gives immediate states.

## Mobile transformation

The grid becomes a single reading column with an optional metadata lane. Practical room and arrival facts precede long prose. Essential content never requires horizontal swiping. Images receive portrait-aware crops; tap targets stay at least 44px.

## Forbidden tendencies

No card field, bento grid, pill taxonomy, repeated centered hero, generic final CTA, decorative coordinates, faux archive, luxury copy, folk ornament, rounded-everything UI, flame gimmicks, or unexplained symbols. Metadata cannot be decorative.

## KAMEN HOUSE specificity

The system treats eleven rooms, seasonal paths, fire-kitchen service, weather, thresholds, and arrival knowledge as primary content. Bulgarian place and culture enter through accurate language, food, climate, and use—not costume.
