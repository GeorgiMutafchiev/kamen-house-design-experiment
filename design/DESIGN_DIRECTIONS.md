# Design Directions

Status: PHASE 2 COMPLETE

## A — The House Register

KAMEN HOUSE behaves like a living guest register: exact, orderly, tactile, and quietly personal. Off-center openings, numbered rooms, annotated documentary images, narrow factual columns, chapter bands, and varied density make hospitality information the visual material. A Cyrillic-capable humanist serif works with a sturdy narrow grotesk. Navigation is a slim visible masthead and “Plan a stay” remains a typographic action. Mobile keeps facts before prose and turns room numbers into a vertical index.

Strength: the content structure itself supplies identity across every required page.  
Risk: “editorial” can decay into decorative metadata, beige prestige, or rules used as theater. Every annotation must answer a guest question.

## B — Weather / Distance / Silence

The stay unfolds through near/far, inside/outside, and morning/evening. A cinematic image sequence is interrupted by small exact blocks. One characterful sans varies through width and weight. An asymmetric eight-column grid and sparse language establish landscape and calm; mobile becomes a deliberate image/text score with practical details moved forward.

Strength: emotionally strong and quiet.  
Risk: depends on exceptional photography and can become a generic eco-lodge fashion film or a magazine with a hotel attached.

## C — Fire Service / Mountain House

A warm public room below and quiet sleeping rooms above. Dense, active kitchen bands alternate with pale room passages. A Cyrillic-capable condensed display face pairs with a humanist sans; temperature and density carry the contrast. Mobile expresses it through type, color, and spacing rather than split screens.

Strength: the most immediately unconventional and warm.  
Risk: can turn the house into a trendy fire restaurant with rooms, or lapse into charcoal/cream/orange cliché.

## Critical comparison and selection

Direction A has the strongest product alignment, accessibility base, and ability to scale across rooms, kitchen, directions, journal, inquiry, privacy, and states without switching visual genres. Direction B has the strongest image-led mood but the highest asset dependency. Direction C has the strongest immediate energy but distorts the balance toward food.

Select Direction A: **The House Register**. Its grammar—register, room number, marginal fact, documentary frame, and chapter band—can vary page rhythm while preserving identity. Its primary danger is editorial costume; sentence-case labels, useful metadata, and a mineral/ember palette are required guardrails.
