# Copy Voice

KAMEN HOUSE sounds like a thoughtful host who knows the building, the kitchen, and the roads. Use concrete nouns, short sentences, exact conditions, and restrained observation. Atmosphere follows information.

Prefer “Eleven rooms above a fire kitchen” to abstract promises. Name what guests need: bed, outlook, breakfast window, road surface, distance, season, ingredients, and access. One plain sentence is often better than a clever heading.

Do not use luxury clichés, generic wellness language, unsupported provenance, fake urgency, testimonials, awards, or claims of real availability. Avoid “escape,” “sanctuary,” “timeless,” “curated,” “crafted with passion,” “where X meets Y,” and “unforgettable.”

Operational details in this fictional experiment must be internally consistent. The inquiry flow explicitly says it sends no real reservation until a backend is connected.
