# Reference Atlas

Status: PHASE 1 COMPLETE  
Purpose: extract transferable principles without copying any site, identity, or signature interaction.

## Research position

KAMEN HOUSE borrows disciplines rather than appearances: hospitality contributes clarity about staying; architecture contributes structure and material restraint; editorial contributes pacing; restaurants contribute operational truth; cultural institutions contribute legible information systems; fashion contributes image direction; independent retail contributes specificity and human scale.

## Cross-category principles

1. Specific information creates atmosphere. Room dimensions, meal times, walking conditions, materials, seasonal changes, and arrival details build trust better than lifestyle claims.
2. Asymmetry needs visible order. Uneven compositions feel authored when alignments, measures, and recurring anchors remain legible.
3. Useful pages may change tempo. Image-led passages can sit beside dense schedules, room facts, and directions without forcing everything into one component pattern.
4. Images behave as evidence. Architecture, food, tools, weather, paths, and traces of use establish place.
5. Navigation remains calm and immediate. Distinction comes from composition and language, not hiding ordinary tasks.
6. Material character emerges through crop, color temperature, type, rule weight, and rhythm—not literal stone textures, flame icons, or folklore ornament.
7. Mobile is recomposed. Hierarchy, captions, image relationships, and inquiry access survive without mechanical stacking.

## Reference fields

### Hospitality

- Introduce the stay through a few concrete details, not a grand promise.
- Give rooms identity through outlook, light, position, material, and use.
- Keep occupancy, bed, bathroom, access, and conditions easy to locate.
- Treat directions, meals, weather, and arrival as hospitality.
- Avoid cinematic-hero/three-card/final-CTA formulas, amenity icon clouds, generic spa imagery, scarcity, and invented availability.

### Architecture

- Use a disciplined 12-column desktop grid with deliberate spans and a recurring caption edge.
- Pair architectural views with junctions, thresholds, surfaces, and occupation.
- Captions state location, orientation, material, or season.
- Prefer flat planes, rules, and alignment to floating cards and decorative depth.
- Avoid random asymmetry, prestige whitespace, and monumental language.

### Editorial

- Build hierarchy through scale, measure, placement, and contrast rather than repeated labels.
- Mix facts, narrative, captions, lists, and reading without one repeated module.
- Journal pages can breathe differently while retaining shared type and navigation.
- Avoid tiny uppercase eyebrows, decorative pull quotes, and false editorial apparatus.

### Restaurants

- Put serving days, hours, reservation expectations, and sample dishes early.
- Use ingredient and process language; menus remain typeset and changeable.
- Photograph work, preparation, vessels, fire, and shared tables.
- Avoid fire effects, chef mythology, generic passion copy, and invented sourcing.

### Cultural institutions

- Separate orientation from discovery with strong titles and contextual routes.
- Treat schedules, seasonal activities, access notes, and closures as first-class content.
- Use visible text links and focus states.
- Avoid unexplained symbols, slow full-screen menus, and missing practical details.

### Fashion and independent retail

- Sequence establishing frames, close crops, details, and negative space.
- Keep chrome quiet where images carry the page; make transitions quick and physical.
- Trust precise nouns, dimensions, provenance, and use.
- Avoid fashion opacity, slow reveals, cursor effects, badges, chips, and simulated ecommerce.

## Type and imagery

Explore a Cyrillic-capable text/display serif with a compact grotesk; a humanist sans across all roles; and a restrained grotesk with a sharp editorial serif at decisive moments. Avoid default luxury pairings, uppercase as hierarchy, and fonts without Bulgarian Cyrillic support.

Use four photographic distances: approach; architectural whole; human activity; material or operational detail. Concept imagery must be identified honestly and must not pretend to document the fictional property.

## Pacing and interaction

Use three tempos: arrival (decisive orientation), residence (slower narrative), and utility (dense facts and actions). Primary destinations stay visible. Rooms and inquiry are separate tasks. Links, rules, crop changes, and small shifts form the interaction vocabulary. Hover is enhancement only; reduced motion is immediate.

## KAMEN HOUSE specificity

- Room register based on position, outlook, light, or material.
- Bulgarian details where language adds meaning.
- Seasonal field notes about paths, snow, kitchen work, and arrival.
- A concise fire-kitchen service sheet.
- Captions that identify parts of the house.
- Directions shaped by local knowledge.
- Structural stone-like type paired with warmer annotations.

Specificity comes through language, food, climate, and practical knowledge—not folk borders, embroidery motifs, faux-rustic letters, or generalized Balkan nostalgia.

## Negative atlas

Avoid the tiny eyebrow/huge centered slogan hero; equal rounded cards; automatic cream-black boutique styling; luxury serif plus neutral sans; alternating image/text bands; amenity icon grids; glass nav; universal pills; giant closing CTAs; ubiquitous scroll reveals; generic poetic cadence; stock-perfect interiors; decorative maps and coordinates; random editorial offsets; fake journal volume; and universal rounded corners.

## Adversarial checks

1. Cover the logo: if another hotel name fits unchanged, specificity is weak.
2. Reduce the page to gray boxes: repeated silhouettes reveal template rhythm.
3. Count rounded containers, pills, icons, centered headings, and three-column layouts.
4. Read headings alone and replace vague promises.
5. Review at 390px without hover.
6. Remove animation; composition must still hold.
7. Practical information must be faster to find than atmospheric claims.
8. Inspect Bulgarian Cyrillic before accepting type.
