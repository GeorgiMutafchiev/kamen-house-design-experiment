# Project Constitution

These rules are immutable unless the human owner explicitly changes them. No subagent may edit this file. Only the main orchestrator may propose a change, and a constitutional change requires explicit human approval.

## Product identity

This remains a hospitality website for KAMEN HOUSE. It must not become SaaS, a marketplace, lifestyle ecommerce, a property-development website, a luxury-resort template, a magazine with a hotel attached, a generic booking engine, or an experimental art project that sacrifices usability.

## Creative objective

The site must be distinctive and human without becoming arbitrary. Originality is not permission for chaos. Restraint is not permission for blandness.

## Anti-AI objective

A technically correct site that visibly uses generic AI-builder visual language is unacceptable.

## Truthfulness

Do not fabricate awards, press, testimonials, reviews, partner logos, occupancy numbers, booking statistics, fake urgency, fake availability, or fake external integrations.

## Usability

The site must remain understandable, navigable, responsive, and accessible.

## Engineering

Do not trade away code quality, accessibility, performance, semantics, or stability merely to generate a striking screenshot.

## Directional preservation

Once a creative direction has been explicitly locked, it may not be replaced casually. Improvement is allowed. Silent reinvention is not.
