## Questions

1. Without being told how it was made, does this site look AI-generated?
   - Yes
   - No
   - Unsure
2. How professionally designed does it feel? (1–10)
3. How distinctive does the business feel? (1–10)
4. How much would you trust this business based on the website? (1–10)
5. Which element most strongly makes it feel human-designed?
6. Which element, if any, makes it feel template- or AI-generated?
7. What feels confusing?
8. What feels unnecessarily designed?

## Context to disclose after the unaided questions

KAMEN HOUSE is a fictional isolated design experiment. The images are generated concept studies, and the inquiry is a non-transmitting interaction preview. This packet evaluates design quality and clarity, not the truth of a real accommodation listing.
