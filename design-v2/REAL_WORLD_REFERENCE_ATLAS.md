## Access evidence — 2026-10-04

Internet access briefly succeeded after Chromium was configured to trust the exact environment proxy CA by SPKI pin. Ace Hotel, Aesop, and eight hospitality homepages returned HTTP 200. Before the required desktop/mobile/interior-page inspections could complete, the environment-wide `cloudflare_https_tunnel` began returning HTTP 503 for every destination, including `example.com`, through both Chromium and `curl`.

The reachable homepages are recorded only as connectivity evidence in `design-v2/NETWORK_RESEARCH_BLOCKER.md`; they are not counted as completed references. No visual observation below may be inferred from model memory. The current evidence gate is in `design-intelligence/research/research-coverage.json`.

Only websites actually opened and inspected in a browser may be described here as live-site observations. Other source categories retain their own evidence and provenance requirements. Model-memory references and fabricated inspection claims are prohibited.

The positive corpus will contain approximately 30–50 professionally designed real-world websites, with at least 70% belonging to real operating commercial organizations. A meaningful subset will be reserved as a validation set and excluded from concept inspiration.

## Required record per reference

- URL and access date
- organization and category
- pages/viewports actually inspected
- why it appears professionally authored
- typography logic
- grid and composition
- whitespace and content density
- image strategy
- navigation and interaction language
- scrolling rhythm
- mobile transformation
- abstract principles worth testing
- what must not be copied literally
