What changes

Nov–Mar

Snow tyres are essential. Some paths are unmarked after fresh snow.

Apr–Jun

Water is high and shade stays cold. Carry a layer even on clear mornings.

Jul–Oct

Start early for the ridge. Afternoon storms can move in fast.
